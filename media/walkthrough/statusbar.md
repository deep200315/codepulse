# 📊 Your Status Bar

Look at the bottom of your VS Code window. You'll see something like:

```
⏱ 2h 30m | 📄 TS 45m | ⌨ 45 WPM
```

- **Active Time** — Total time you've been coding today
- **Language** — Current language and time spent in it
- **WPM** — Your live typing speed

**Click it** to see a full breakdown of today's stats!
